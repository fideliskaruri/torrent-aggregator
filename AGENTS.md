## Per-page owner expectations (owner''s words — filled in as we go)
- **1. Home (`/`):** **Stable, deliberate categories, never random** (owner, 1 Oct: "The home page has been looking random most of the time. Let's work on showing different categories of films and all that."). One server list, `HomeRails` (`server/TorrentFlow.Metadata/Browse/HomeRails.cs`), names every rail; the web renders what `GET /api/browse` names and knows only the four type routes (`web/src/lib/browse/views.ts`). **All** (`/`): Downloaded, Because you're watching, then Popular films, Popular series, Anime this season, New films, New series, Trending anime, Top rated films and Top rated series (TMDB key only), Top rated anime. A link-based type switch (All, Films, Series, Anime; `/?type=films`) shows that type's three featured rails, then one rail per genre. Genre chips and a genre rail's **See all** open `/?type=films&genre=comedy`, a grid of up to 48 with no hero. Rails refresh **once per local day** with a sticky provider: a failed refresh keeps yesterday's rows, and nothing is shuffled. Every rail is full and no title shows twice. **Rows lead with what can be fetched now** (owner, 1 Oct: the hero showed a film still in cinemas): the card gate that draws "In cinemas" and "Coming" (`ReleaseGates.Gate`) moves films in cinemas to one **In cinemas** row, last on All and Films, drops films not out yet, and puts unaired series and anime at the end of their row. The hero is the first title available now (Because you're watching first), never In cinemas, Coming or Unreleased. Without TMDB release dates a film counts as in cinemas for 21 days after release. Without a TMDB key, films and series come from Cinemeta and anime from AniList. Plan, journeys and proof: `docs/home-categories-plan.md`.
- **2. Search (`/search`):** Search must be **fuzzy / typo- & whitespace-tolerant** (finds the title despite misspellings/extra spaces). Results are **unified** — films, series and anime surface together for one query, NOT siloed behind category tabs you must switch between (owner: tabs-as-a-wall is the complaint; BUG-007 open for final contract confirmation). No vestigial dead "Open search" screen. Clicking a result must load its title page (never "Unsupported title provider").

- **5. Title (`/title/[workKey]`):** Episode list must not flash on the 5s poll. Every season the user selects must show THAT season's episodes (from the provider when there are no local files) — never a blank list. **Season selection persists in the bounded per-title `tf_season` cookie; title URLs stay free of `?s=`.** Every episode card reports its exact episode torrent/file transfer, never season-level or inferred pack progress. Download is offered honestly. **No in-app playback or streaming:** a film already on this computer offers **Open** (in the owner's own player) and **Show in folder** (`POST /api/downloads/{id}/open`, see `docs/api-open-file.md`), and a finished episode card offers **Open** in place of its Download pill (not for an episode held only inside a season pack); requesters see neither, and nothing tracks what was watched. The season header carries one line, "12 of 38 downloaded · 3 downloading" (`seasonProgressLabel`), counted over every episode row rather than only the 200 rendered; the hero sums the seasons in the same words and ring (`showProgressFromSeasons` + `SeasonProgressMeter`). No Episodes jump button.
  **A selected download quality is a hard minimum, not a fallback preference:** lower and unknown resolutions are ineligible; a higher resolution may be used when no exact-quality release exists. Monitored automation must keep the episode cursor pinned when only sub-floor releases exist.
- **6. Downloads (`/downloads`):** **Every Download is fire and forget** (owner's rule, `docs/rules.md`): pressing **Download** on a film or a single episode, or **Download season**, answers at once ("Looking for Sintel.", "Season N queued.") and never shows an error or waits on a dead release; the title page shows the row as **Looking** until a release starts. The background wanted queue (`WantedQueue`) runs the shared grab (`GrabService.Grab` for a film, `GrabService.Season` for episodes: exactly the single-episode grab run once per episode, no season-pack preference, owner's explicit rule), runs queued seasons side by side (a clicked film or episode never waits for a season slot), and keeps retrying whatever isn't found yet. Only when retries keep finding nothing does the owner get one notification with **Pick a release yourself**. Approved requests use the same queue. **Season packs only as a last-resort fallback** (owner's rule, `docs/season-pack-fallback.md` v1, behind `TorrentFlow:Library:SeasonPackFallback`, default off): never as an episode candidate, never in the picker; only from the wanted queue after an episode really missed twice, only for a season the provider proves has finished, file list inspected and strictly mapped first, only the missing episodes' files downloaded. When an episode isn't found, the real reason is recorded per episode (search refused, no results, or which filter turned the results down, e.g. "12 results, all season packs") in the Activity log and `GET /api/wanted` (`why`). **Delete** = gone immediately, no caching/lingering after the confirm dialog. **No banner** ("Active Now" band removed).
  **Series detail is a scrollable dialog, not an inline accordion** (this supersedes any earlier "expand a show → Season N → episode cards inline" wording — BUG-004 is the owner naming that shallow rearrangement for what it was; do not revert to it). The main page stays compact: one `SeriesOverviewRow` per work — poster/title, honest combined state/progress/counts/size, a selection checkbox, an explicit **Details** button (`data-group-details`) that opens the dialog, and an overflow for whole-series pause/resume/delete. Series rows state their tally in the title page's words, without a total, with episodes waiting in the queue counted apart from the one moving ("6 downloaded · 1 downloading · 2 queued"). Films keep their own direct row (never routed through the dialog): a finished film says **Ready** once, with **Open** beside it and **Show in folder** in its overflow. Every row shows its progress bar only while unfinished, and says **Files missing** when the files are gone from disk.
  **Page top:** one quiet status line (`data-downloads-summary`: Connected · N downloads · N downloading · N queued · N ready · N paused · up to [stepper] at once, counted by the same `statusCounts` the rows use; a **Queued** status chip appears while anything waits), where the stepper is `MaxActiveDownloadsControl`, the same control Settings uses. **Add torrent** is a panel, collapsed by default and remembered per browser (`tf-downloads-add-open`); a **Sort** select (Recent by default, Name, Quality, Size, Progress) is remembered too (`tf-downloads-sort`). Folders live in Settings, not here.
  Clicking **Details** opens `SeriesDownloadDialog` (`web/src/app/downloads/series-download-dialog.tsx`), built on the general-purpose Radix Dialog primitive (`web/src/components/ui/dialog.tsx` — distinct from `alert-dialog.tsx`, which stays reserved for yes/no destructive confirms). Layout: full-height sheet on mobile, centered/capped on desktop (`max-h-90dvh`). Header = poster/title/aggregate progress-counts-size-speed + close + whole-series overflow. Immediately below, a **shrink-0 horizontally-scrollable season rail** (`data-season-rail`, `role="tablist"`, one `role="tab"` per season with arrow-key/Home/End navigation, a percent readout and a thin truthful per-season progress fill) — this rail is the redesign's signature. Only the body below it (`data-season-panel`) scrolls vertically; nothing in the ancestor chain sets `overflow-x: hidden` (breaks the rail's scroll + any sticky header). Episode/release cards (`data-episode-card`) render one column below `md`, two at `md+`: identity, one quality chip + source, state badge, exact percent + progress bar while unfinished, size, live speed/ETA/peers, a completed check, **Open** on a finished single-episode file (a pack's file only gets Show in folder), and an overflow (Show in folder or Open folder, pause/resume — only the one the state admits, via `pauseResumeAction`, and neither on a finished built-in download — delete, raw release/path details) — plus a modal-scoped selected-action bar when episodes are multi-selected. Each season panel starts with a sticky tri-state **Select all (N)** row (`data-season-select-all`: none / some / all) that feeds the page's one `toggleGroupSelect`.
  **Data contract:** the dialog always derives from every download row for that work, independent of the page's search/status/media-tab filters — narrowing those filters must never hide a season/episode from an already-open dialog. Default season = the first actively-downloading/incomplete season, else the first ordered season; an already-picked season survives every 5s poll. If the group disappears (deleted / filtered to nothing), the dialog closes itself and announces it. See `season-selection.ts` for the pure default/poll-stability helpers.
